Do I have to be a Christian to work with you?

No. Dr. Arnaud’s care is rooted in Christian faith, and everyone is welcome. Faith is part of your sessions only as much as you want it to be.

Is my problem “serious enough” for counseling?

You don’t need a crisis to ask for help. Stress, conflict, worry or feeling stuck are all good reasons to talk. The free 15-minute call is a no-pressure way to find out if counseling fits.

How do you combine faith with proven methods?

Sessions use methods with strong research behind them, like CBT and DBT. When you want it, they also include prayer, Scripture and conversations about faith. You decide the balance.

What happens in the free call and the first session?

In the free call, you share a little about what’s going on and ask your questions. If it feels like a good fit, you book a first session. There, you talk about your story and your goals, and you make a plan together.

How do online sessions work?

You meet Dr. Arnaud by video from a quiet, private place. All you need is a phone or computer with a camera and a good internet connection. Online sessions are available across the U.S. and in Brazil.

Is everything I share confidential?

Yes. What you share stays private. By law, there are a few exceptions, such as when someone is in danger or a child is being harmed. Dr. Arnaud explains these clearly in your first session.

Do you work with teens? Do parents take part?

Yes. Teens get their own safe space to talk, and parents are part of the process. For teens under 18, a parent or guardian gives consent, and Dr. Arnaud explains what will be shared with parents.

How much does it cost? Do you take insurance?

Fees depend on the type of session. We’ll go over them in your free call, and you can ask about insurance there too.

Can you lead a workshop or speak at our church?

Yes. Dr. Arnaud leads workshops and talks for churches, nonprofits and volunteer teams, in person or online, in English or Portuguese. Request a workshop.
