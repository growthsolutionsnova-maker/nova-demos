Trauma counseling

Christian Trauma Counseling in Boca Raton & Online

Trauma can keep your body and mind on alert long after the danger is gone. Heal at your own pace, with gentle, faith-centered care.

Book your free 15-min call (954) 899-3979

Does this sound like you?

If one or more of these feels familiar, counseling can help.

  • You feel on edge, jumpy or numb
  • Memories, nightmares or triggers keep coming back
  • You react strongly and don’t know why
  • Relationships feel hard since something happened
  • Your family went through something painful together

How counseling helps

Faith and proven methods, at a pace that feels right for you.

  • Feel safe first

    Learn how trauma affects the body, and tools to calm your nervous system.

  • Heal at your pace

    Work through what happened gently, with methods like CBT, DBT and somatic tools.

  • Hope and meaning

    Bring your faith into healing, if you want to.

What to expect

  1. Free 15-minute call. Share what’s going on and ask your questions.
  2. First session. Talk about your story and your goals.
  3. Your plan. Agree on next steps and how often to meet.

Common questions

Ask anything else in your free call.

Do I have to talk about everything that happened?

No. You decide what to share and when. Feeling safe always comes first.

Can you help children and families?

Yes. Dr. Arnaud works with families and children affected by traumatic stress.

What if I’m in crisis?

If you are in danger or thinking about hurting yourself, call or text 988, or call 911. Counseling is not emergency care.
